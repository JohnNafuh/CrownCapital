1. Who we are

[Company legal name], doing business as Crown Capital, runs this website and is responsible for your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and we never see them in plain text.
  • Investment activity: the plans you choose, amounts, dates, balances and payouts.
  • Payment information: the payment method you choose. Card and bank details are handled by our payment partners; we don't store full card numbers.
  • Settings: your notification preferences.
  • Device and usage data: basic information such as browser type, sign-in times and pages visited, used to keep the service secure and working.

3. How we use it

  • to create and run your account and plans,
  • to process payments and withdrawals,
  • to send account emails such as verification, password resets and payout notices,
  • to send optional updates you've switched on in Account settings,
  • to prevent fraud and meet legal obligations, and
  • to improve the website.

We don't sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run Crown Capital:

  • Service providers who host our website and data, handle sign-in and send email (for example, Google Firebase),
  • Payment partners who process deposits and withdrawals,
  • Authorities when the law requires it or to protect our users from fraud.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use essential browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We don't use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open and for as long afterwards as the law requires for financial records. After that, we delete or anonymize it.

7. Keeping it safe

We use encrypted connections, secure sign-in and access controls so that only you can see your account data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

8. Your choices and rights

You can update your name and notification settings in Account. You can also ask us to send you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account by emailing support@crowncapital.com.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you may have extra rights, such as knowing what we collect and asking us to delete it. We won't treat you differently for using them.

9. Children

Crown Capital is for adults only. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we'll delete the account.

10. Where your data is stored

Our providers may store and process data in the United States and other countries. Wherever it's stored, we protect it as described in this policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we'll change the date at the top, and for important changes we'll tell you by email or on the website.
